      How to Tune a Head

      1. Seat the head: press the centre firmly with your palm a few times so it settles on the bearing edge.
      2. Finger-tighten every rod evenly until each one just touches the hoop.
      3. Tighten in a star (criss-cross) pattern, the same small turn on every rod: half turns at first, then quarter turns as you get close.
      4. Tap about an inch in from each rod and listen, or use the tuner. Bring any lug that sounds off up or down to match the others.
      5. Tune the resonant head the same way, then choose how it relates to the batter head.
      6. Play the drum with the rest of the kit and adjust by ear.
